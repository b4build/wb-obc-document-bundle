# Google Form draft: West Bengal OBC public information and evidence desk

## Form title

**West Bengal OBC: report a certificate issue, case update, or source**

## Form description

This public-interest research project collects verifiable information about West Bengal OBC administration, court cases, and related public documents. Your report may help us identify missing records, verify a timeline entry, or understand recurring implementation problems.

Please share only information you are permitted to share. Do **not** upload Aadhaar numbers, caste-certificate numbers, addresses, phone numbers, signatures, bank details, private pleadings, or any other sensitive personal information. Remove or cover personal details from photographs and documents before uploading them.

We will treat every report as a lead, not as an established fact. We may publish a summary only after checking the available evidence and will avoid publishing personal information.

For urgent legal or administrative relief, contact the relevant authority or a qualified legal adviser. This form is not a complaint portal and does not provide legal advice.

## Form settings to use

- Do not collect email addresses automatically; keep the optional contact field.
- Do not require sign-in if anonymous reports are wanted. Note: Google Forms file upload requires sign-in. If that is unacceptable, ask contributors to submit a public source URL instead.
- Use response validation for URLs where appropriate.
- Turn on a confirmation message: `Thank you. Your report is a research lead and will be checked against primary sources where possible. This form does not create an official complaint or legal case.`
- Restrict edit access to trusted project maintainers and export periodically to a protected sheet.
- Set a review and deletion policy before collecting responses.
